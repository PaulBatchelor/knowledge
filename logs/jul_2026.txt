#! dz thoughts/projects/sonilo/tasks/bigverb
I'm looking into memory requirements for bigverb.
---
The extra memory allocated is 24684 samples. That's 24k words!
---
napkin math: 24.6k words is approximately 386 blocks, or 37 percent
of the total sonilo memory space. In extended space, it would take up
about 97 blocks.
---
Interesting thought: when the samplerate is 16khz, there are 9036 samples
required. Perhaps there could be a way to resample to the host sample
rate (this would be nice to know how to do).
---
Lowering the bit depth: 9036 14-bit samples comes out to about 3953 words,
which is very close to 4096, or 64 blocks. It is 16 megablocks.
---
12khz is 6807 samples, which is just under 3k words with 14-bit audio.
10khz is 5695 samples, which is just under 2.5k words with 14-bit audio.
---
I am thinking the winning combo might be 12khz/14-bit audio, which would
be 2978 samples, with packed 14-bit audio that's about 12 megablocks.
