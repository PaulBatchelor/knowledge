I think I've done all the work I wanted to get done for
this class. The pearson calculus outline is incomplete,
but it's not worth my time. I could have transcribed some
of the practice midterm/final question. But, I already have
a pretty good set of practice problems.
---
The final was a bit of a gut punch. It was a little more
challenging than was initial implied. I did not like that much of
the content on the exam was hastily covered in the last two
lectures of class, without any assigned problem sets or discussion
to practice the concepts. It leaves a bitter taste. I also don't
know how I would have learned this stuff covered on the exam
with the supplied Pearson curriculum. It was very much an exam
designed by the instructor with their views on the subject,
with little regard for how the content was explained in
the textbook.
---
I will say this was a much, much, better class experience than
my previous semester. Was it worth the cost, though? It's
hard to say right now. I paid more than twice the cost of last semester,
for half the credits. That's a lot of money.
I got more value, but how much more value?
---
I think my lasting impression of this class is: when you're
in a math class that's 100+ students, you're on your own.
Seek out the good textbooks and teachers. You might as well
learn it the right way.
---
Okay. Signing off now. Linear algebra (possibly) awaits me
in a little over a week.
